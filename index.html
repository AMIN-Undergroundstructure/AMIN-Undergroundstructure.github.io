Amin AI Hub Drive

Amin AI Hub Drive is a personal Google Drive integration used by its owner to manage selected files and securely store encrypted backups of a private automation environment.

Access is granted through Google OAuth by the account holder. The application requests the drive.file permission to work only with specific files it creates or is authorised to access. It is not a public file-sharing or commercial service.

The intended backup workflow encrypts sensitive backup content before it is transferred to Google Drive. This website does not upload files, request a Google login, or collect personal data through a form.
