Privacy Policy

Amin AI Hub Drive · Effective 20 October 2026

Purpose and operator

Amin AI Hub Drive is a private, owner-operated Google Drive integration, not a public service. It is designed to store and retrieve selected files, including backups of the owner's personal automation environment.

Google data accessed

The application requests the Google Drive https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This permits access to files created by the application or specifically authorised for use with it. The integration does not request permission to read the entire Google Drive account.

Use and sharing

Google Drive access is used only to create, upload, verify, download, and maintain the owner's selected files and backups. Drive content is not sold, used for advertising, used to train AI models, or shared with independent third parties through this application. Google processes files stored in Drive under its own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and security

The intended backup process encrypts sensitive backup archives before upload. Google OAuth tokens and backup decryption credentials are to be stored privately, not published on this website or in its public source repository. No claim is made here that a particular offsite backup or full restore has already been completed.

Retention and deletion

Files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them. The account holder may revoke this application's access through Google Account security settings and may delete uploaded files in Google Drive. Revoking access does not automatically delete files already stored in Drive.

Website privacy

This informational website has no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies provided by the developer. As the hosting provider, GitHub Pages may process standard site-access information according to GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

For questions or requests related to this application, use the developer contact shown on its Google OAuth consent screen or visit the developer's GitHub profile.
